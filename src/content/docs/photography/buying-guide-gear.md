  <section class="scroll-mt-24 space-y-5 border-t border-white/10 pt-10" id="first-questions">
    <p class="m-0! font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.75rem] font-bold tracking-[0.18em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Before choosing a model</p>
    <h2 class="mt-0! border-0! pt-0!">Answer these four questions</h2>
    <p>
      Your priorities depend on your budget and the photos you take. Sports, street, and travel photographers need different things.
    </p>
    <div class="grid gap-px overflow-hidden border border-white/10 bg-white/10 sm:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4" aria-label="Questions to answer before buying">
      <div class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.72rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Budget</span>
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">How much can you spend?</strong>
        <p>Include the lens, batteries, memory cards, and a bag. A cheaper body with a good lens is often the better buy.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.72rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Subject</span>
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">What do you shoot?</strong>
        <p>Sports and wildlife need fast autofocus. Street and travel work well with small kits. Portraits and events often need better lenses and low-light performance.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.72rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Size</span>
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">Will you carry it often?</strong>
        <p>A small camera you carry is more useful than a better camera left at home.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.72rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Mount</span>
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">Which lenses fit?</strong>
        <p>The lens mount controls which lenses you can use. Check lens prices before choosing a body, especially for a full-frame system.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="grid scroll-mt-24 overflow-hidden border border-white/10 bg-neutral-900 lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,0.9fr)_minmax(0,1.1fr)]" id="used-gear">
    <div class="space-y-5 border-b border-white/10 p-6 sm:p-8 lg:border-r lg:border-b-0">
      <p class="m-0! font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.75rem] font-bold tracking-[0.18em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Used gear</p>
      <h3 class="mt-0!">A used camera can save money</h3>
      <p>
        Many older cameras still work well. Compare used prices on sites such as eBay, MPB, and KEH. Check the condition, shutter count if listed, return policy, included battery and charger, lens mount, and sensor.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="grid gap-px bg-white/10 sm:grid-cols-3 lg:grid-cols-1" aria-label="Used camera checklist">
      <div class="space-y-2 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">Check the listing</strong>
        <p>Confirm the model, included accessories, visible wear, and any listed damage.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">Check the system</strong>
        <p>Check lens prices before buying the body. A cheap body is not a bargain if its lenses cost too much.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <strong class="block text-neutral-100!">Check the return policy</strong>
        <p>Buy from a seller with a good rating and a clear return policy.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="scroll-mt-24 space-y-5 border-t border-white/10 pt-10" id="what-matters">
    <p class="m-0! font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.75rem] font-bold tracking-[0.18em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Camera features</p>
    <h2 class="mt-0! border-0! pt-0!">What to check first</h2>
    <div class="grid gap-px overflow-hidden border border-white/10 bg-white/10 sm:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4" aria-label="Camera buying priorities">
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-amber-200/[0.06] p-5 ring-1 ring-inset ring-amber-200/30">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-amber-200 uppercase">Start here</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">Cost</h3>
        <p>Choose a camera that fits your full budget, including the lens and accessories.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">Mount</h3>
        <p>The mount controls which lenses fit. Most brands use their own mount, and third-party support differs.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">DSLR or mirrorless</h3>
        <p>DSLRs use mirrors and an optical viewfinder. Mirrorless cameras use an electronic viewfinder and often cost more.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">Autofocus</h3>
        <p>Basic autofocus works for still subjects. Sports, events, wildlife, and fast action need better tracking.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">Sensor size</h3>
        <p>Smaller sensors often use smaller, cheaper lenses. Larger sensors can help in low light and give more depth-of-field control, but the system costs more.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">IBIS</h3>
        <p>In-body image stabilization lets you use slower shutter speeds by hand. It reduces camera shake, not subject motion.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="space-y-3 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.7rem] font-bold tracking-[0.14em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Important</span>
        <h3 class="mt-0!">Size and weight</h3>
        <p>Smaller, lighter gear is easier to carry and may help you take more photos.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="scroll-mt-24 border-t border-white/10 pt-10" id="nice-specialized">
    <div class="grid gap-10 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:gap-12">
      <div class="space-y-5">
        <p class="m-0! font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.75rem] font-bold tracking-[0.18em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Nice to have</p>
        <h2 class="mt-0! border-0! pt-0!">Useful, but not essential</h2>
        <div class="grid gap-px overflow-hidden border border-white/10 bg-white/10 *:space-y-2 *:bg-neutral-950 *:p-4 [&_strong]:block" aria-label="Nice to have camera features">
          <div><strong>Screen type</strong><p>A fully articulating screen helps with video and unusual angles. A tilting screen is quicker to move.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Battery life</strong><p>Long battery life helps, but you can carry a spare battery.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Weather sealing</strong><p>It helps in rain and dust, but the lens also needs sealing. Most cameras are not waterproof.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Viewfinder</strong><p>A good viewfinder helps with fast action and bright daylight. Some people prefer the rear screen.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Grip and controls</strong><p>Try the camera in your hands. Comfortable controls make it easier to use.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Megapixels</strong><p>Most cameras have enough. More pixels help when you crop heavily or make large prints.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Dynamic range</strong><p>More range helps recover bright and dark detail from Raw files, especially in harsh light.</p></div>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-5">
        <p class="m-0! font-['Space_Mono',monospace] text-[0.75rem] font-bold tracking-[0.18em] text-emerald-300 uppercase">Specialized</p>
        <h2 class="mt-0! border-0! pt-0!">Features for specific work</h2>
        <div class="grid gap-px overflow-hidden border border-white/10 bg-white/10 *:space-y-2 *:bg-neutral-950 *:p-4 [&_strong]:block" aria-label="Specialized camera features">
          <div><strong>Pixel-shift modes</strong><p>Combine several shifted frames for more detail. They work best with still scenes.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Buffer</strong><p>Holds burst photos before writing them to the card. This matters for sports and wildlife.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Rolling shutter</strong><p>Can distort fast movement, especially in video and action photos.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Burst rate</strong><p>A high frame rate helps with sports, wildlife, and other fast sequences. Most beginners do not need it.</p></div>
          <div><strong>Dual card slots</strong><p>Save a second copy for professional work. Most beginners need only one slot.</p></div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
