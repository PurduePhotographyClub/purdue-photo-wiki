  <section class="anatomy-section anatomy-control-section scroll-mt-24 space-y-8 border-t border-neutral-800 pt-10" id="control-map">
    <div class="anatomy-control-section__intro max-w-3xl space-y-5">
      <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-sky-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-sky-200">Top controls</p>
      <h2>The Dials Decide How You Shoot</h2>
      <p>
        The top plate is where most cameras put the controls you change while shooting. The exact layout varies, but the pattern is usually the same: one dial chooses the shooting mode, one or two dials change exposure settings, and a few buttons trigger capture or quick actions.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="anatomy-control-section__body grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:items-start">
      <div class="anatomy-label-grid anatomy-control-list grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-800 lg:grid-cols-2 [&_div]:space-y-2 [&_div]:border-b [&_div]:border-r [&_div]:border-neutral-800 [&_div]:bg-neutral-950 [&_div]:p-5 [&_p]:text-sm [&_p]:leading-6 [&_p]:text-neutral-400 [&_strong]:block [&_strong]:font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] [&_strong]:text-lg [&_strong]:text-neutral-100" aria-label="Top camera controls">
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-sky-400/60 bg-sky-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-sky-200">1</span>
          <strong>Mode dial</strong>
          <p>Switches between Auto, P, A, S, M, video, and custom modes. If you read <a href="/photography/basics/#camera-modes">Basics</a>, this is where those modes live.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-amber-400/60 bg-amber-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-amber-200">2</span>
          <strong>Front dial</strong>
          <p>Usually changes aperture, shutter speed, or exposure compensation depending on the mode.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-emerald-400/60 bg-emerald-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-emerald-200">3</span>
          <strong>Rear dial</strong>
          <p>Usually changes the second exposure setting, such as shutter speed in manual mode or exposure compensation in aperture priority.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-rose-400/60 bg-rose-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-rose-200">4</span>
          <strong>Extra dial</strong>
          <p>Not every camera has this. On bodies that do, it can often be customized for ISO, exposure compensation, or another frequent setting.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-sky-400/60 bg-sky-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-sky-200">5</span>
          <strong>Hot shoe</strong>
          <p>Mounts accessories such as a flash, trigger, microphone, or wireless transmitter. A shoe without electronic contacts is a cold shoe.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-amber-400/60 bg-amber-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-amber-200">6</span>
          <strong>Record button</strong>
          <p>Starts video recording. Some cameras let you reassign it if you rarely shoot video.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

  <section class="anatomy-section anatomy-control-section scroll-mt-24 space-y-8 border-t border-neutral-800 pt-10" id="back-controls">

      <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-rose-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-rose-200">Rear controls</p>
      <h2>The Back Handles Focus, Review, and Navigation</h2>
      <p>
        The back of the camera is where you compose, move focus, review images, and reach the menu. You do not need every button immediately. Learn the viewfinder, screen, joystick, playback button, and whatever control opens your quick menu.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="anatomy-control-section__body anatomy-control-section__body--reverse grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:items-start">
      <figure class="anatomy-figure anatomy-figure--light border border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-3 lg:sticky lg:top-24">
        <img class="aspect-[5/4] w-full bg-neutral-100 object-contain" src="/images/pages/anatomy-of-a-camera/back-controls.webp" alt="Back view of a Sony A7 IV with numbered arrows pointing to the viewfinder, rear screen, AF-ON, AEL, joystick, wheel, playback, delete, and strap mount." width="1500" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
        <figcaption>Rear controls on a Sony A7 IV reference body.</figcaption>
      </figure>
      <div class="anatomy-label-grid anatomy-label-grid--compact anatomy-control-list grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-800 lg:grid-cols-2 [&_div]:space-y-2 [&_div]:border-b [&_div]:border-r [&_div]:border-neutral-800 [&_div]:bg-neutral-950 [&_div]:p-5 [&_p]:text-sm [&_p]:leading-6 [&_p]:text-neutral-400 [&_strong]:block [&_strong]:font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] [&_strong]:text-lg [&_strong]:text-neutral-100" aria-label="Rear camera controls">
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-sky-400/60 bg-sky-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-sky-200">7</span>
          <strong>Viewfinder</strong>
          <p>A small screen you hold to your eye for composing. It is a holdover from film and DSLR cameras, but it still helps in bright light and when you want to block out everything except the frame.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-amber-400/60 bg-amber-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-amber-200">8</span>
          <strong>Rear screen</strong>
          <p>Used for live view, menus, playback, touch focus, and shooting from angles where the viewfinder is awkward.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-emerald-400/60 bg-emerald-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-emerald-200">9</span>
          <strong>AF-ON</strong>
          <p>Activates autofocus. Some photographers use this for back-button focus so focusing and taking the photo are separate actions.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-rose-400/60 bg-rose-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-rose-200">10</span>
          <strong>AEL</strong>
          <p>Auto Exposure Lock freezes the camera's metered exposure so it does not change while you recompose.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-sky-400/60 bg-sky-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-sky-200">11</span>
          <strong>Joystick</strong>
          <p>Moves the autofocus point or navigates menus. Some beginner bodies use the rear wheel or touchscreen instead.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid min-h-11 min-w-11 place-items-center border border-amber-400/60 bg-amber-400/10 px-2 text-xs font-bold text-amber-200">12-14</span>
          <strong>Wheel + review</strong>
          <p>The control wheel navigates menus. Playback opens your gallery. Delete removes photos and often doubles as a custom button while shooting.</p>
        </div>
        <div>
          <span class="anatomy-number inline-grid size-11 place-items-center border border-emerald-400/60 bg-emerald-400/10 text-xs font-bold text-emerald-200">15</span>
          <strong>Strap mount</strong>
          <p>One of the anchor points for a strap. Boring until the day it saves your camera from the floor.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="anatomy-band scroll-mt-24 grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 border border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-900 p-5 sm:p-7 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:items-center lg:p-8" id="beginner-layout">
    <div class="space-y-5">
      <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-emerald-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-emerald-200">A clean beginner setup</p>
      <h3>Aperture Priority + Auto ISO Is Enough for a Lot</h3>
      <p>
        For portraits, campus walks, events, travel, and most everyday shooting, try <span class="anatomy-term anatomy-term--green font-bold text-emerald-300">Aperture Priority</span> with <span class="anatomy-term anatomy-term--amber font-bold text-amber-300">Auto ISO</span>. Use the front dial for aperture, use exposure compensation when the image is too bright or dark, and let the camera handle shutter speed inside sensible limits.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="anatomy-mini-grid grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-700 lg:grid-cols-2" aria-label="Beginner camera setup">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-700 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-emerald-300">A / Av</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">You pick aperture</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Controls depth of field and how much light the lens lets in.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-700 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-300">Auto ISO</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Camera fills the gap</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Raises sensitivity only when aperture and shutter need help.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-700 bg-neutral-950 p-5 lg:col-span-2">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-rose-300">Exposure comp</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Make it brighter or darker</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Tell the meter when the scene should not be middle gray.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="anatomy-section scroll-mt-24 space-y-6 border-t border-neutral-800 pt-10" id="body-parts">
    <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-amber-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-amber-200">Body parts</p>
    <h2>The Physical Pieces That Matter</h2>
    <p>
      The body is more than a box with buttons. These parts decide what lenses fit, how light reaches the sensor, how the camera saves files, and what accessories it can use.
    </p>
    <div class="anatomy-component-grid grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-800 lg:grid-cols-2" aria-label="Physical camera parts">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-300">Lens mount</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Where the lens locks on</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">The mount holds the lens at the correct distance from the sensor and lets the body communicate with autofocus, aperture, and stabilization electronics.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-sky-300">Sensor</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Where light becomes data</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">The sensor records the image. Larger sensors often give more low-light flexibility and depth-of-field control, but lens choice and technique still matter.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-rose-300">Shutter</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">How long light is recorded</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">A mechanical or electronic shutter controls exposure time. This is the physical side of the shutter speed you learn in Basics.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-emerald-300">Hot shoe</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Accessory connection</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Use it for flash, triggers, microphones, and other accessories. Some brands also pass digital audio or control data through the shoe.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-sky-300">Card slot</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Where files are saved</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Fast cards help with bursts and video. Format cards in camera after backing them up so the file system stays clean.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
        <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-300">Battery</span>
        <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">The real limiting spec</strong>
        <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Mirrorless cameras use power quickly because the screen, viewfinder, autofocus, and stabilization are active while you shoot.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="anatomy-section anatomy-section--split scroll-mt-24 grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 border-t border-neutral-800 pt-10 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:items-center" id="viewfinder-screen">
    <div class="anatomy-section__copy space-y-5">
      <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-sky-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-sky-200">Finder and screen</p>
      <h2>Two Ways to Compose the Same Photo</h2>
      <p>
        The <span class="anatomy-term anatomy-term--cyan font-bold text-sky-300">viewfinder</span> is a holdover from film and DSLR days, but it still has advantages over using the screen like a phone. It can make shooting feel more immersive by blocking out everything except your frame, it can be easier to see in bright daylight, and on many cameras it can run brighter or at a higher refresh rate than the rear screen. Some people simply prefer the experience.
      </p>
      <p>
        Viewfinder placement also changes the camera's shape. Many cameras place it in the middle of the body like a DSLR. Rangefinder-style cameras usually have a flatter top plate and place the viewfinder off to the left side.
      </p>
      <p>
        The <span class="anatomy-term anatomy-term--amber font-bold text-amber-300">rear screen</span> is better for low angles, high angles, video, menus, and touch focus. Fully articulating screens can flip to the side and rotate, which is useful for awkward compositions and filming yourself.
      </p>
      <div class="anatomy-summary-grid grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-800 lg:grid-cols-2" aria-label="Viewfinder and screen comparison">
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-sky-300">Viewfinder</span>
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Eye-level focus</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Best for bright daylight, steady handholding, and immersive framing.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <span class="text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-wider text-amber-300">Rear screen</span>
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Flexible angles</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Best for touch controls, low/high perspectives, video, and tripod work.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

  <section class="anatomy-section anatomy-section--split anatomy-section--reverse scroll-mt-24 grid grid-cols-1 gap-8 border-t border-neutral-800 pt-10 lg:grid-cols-2 lg:items-center" id="important-settings">
    <div class="anatomy-section__copy space-y-5 lg:order-2">
      <p class="anatomy-kicker inline-flex min-h-11 items-center border-l-2 border-emerald-400 pl-4 text-[0.7rem] font-bold uppercase tracking-[0.22em] text-emerald-200">Important settings</p>
      <h2>Settings Worth Finding Early</h2>
      <p>
        Modern cameras have deep menus, but a beginner does not need to master all of them. These settings change day-to-day shooting more than most hidden menu items.
      </p>
      <div class="anatomy-settings-list grid grid-cols-1 border-l border-t border-neutral-800 lg:grid-cols-2" aria-label="Important beginner camera settings">
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">IBIS / stabilization</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Leave it on for handheld shooting. It reduces blur from your hands, especially at slower shutter speeds, but it cannot freeze a moving subject.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Autofocus mode</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Use single AF for still subjects and continuous AF for people, events, sports, wildlife, or anything moving toward or away from you.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Subject detection</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">If your camera has eye, face, animal, vehicle, or bird detection, assign it to a quick button or menu so you can change it without digging.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-r border-neutral-800 bg-neutral-950 p-5">
          <strong class="block font-['Playfair_Display',Georgia,serif] text-lg text-neutral-100">Raw / JPEG</strong>
          <p class="text-sm leading-6 text-neutral-400">Shoot Raw if you want more editing flexibility. Shoot JPEG when you want smaller files and finished-looking images straight from camera.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
